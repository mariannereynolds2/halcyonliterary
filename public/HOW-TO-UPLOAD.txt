HOW TO UPLOAD (Hostinger or similar)
  1. Log in to your hosting and open File Manager, then the public_html folder.
  2. FIRST, download a backup: select everything in public_html, compress it
     to a zip and download it. Keep that zip somewhere safe (and on GitHub!).
  3. DO NOT delete the existing "assets" folder on the server. The bookshelf
     photo, the 5 collaborator photos and the roadmap PDF live there.
  4. Upload everything from this folder into public_html (including the css,
     js, i18n and assets folders). When asked, allow it to replace files and
     MERGE the assets folder, so the new covers and team photos are added next
     to what is already there.
  5. Open halcyonliterary.com and refresh (Ctrl+F5) to see the new design.

LANGUAGES
  The whole site is translated into 11 languages, built in (no Google needed):
  Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese,
  Hindi, Swahili and Yoruba.
  - The site picks the language by the visitor's COUNTRY. Someone opening it in
    Spain sees Spanish, in France French, in Brazil Portuguese, in Japan
    Japanese, in Egypt Arabic, in the USA or UK English, and so on.
    The country is looked up once (free GeoJS service) and remembered for 30 days.
  - Countries with several languages (Nigeria, India, Kenya, Canada,
    Switzerland, Belgium, South Africa, Singapore...) follow the visitor's own
    phone or computer language instead. So a Nigerian visitor whose phone is
    set to Yoruba sees Yoruba; everyone else in Nigeria sees English.
  - If the country can't be found, the visitor's phone/computer language is
    used, then English.
  - The globe menu (inside the Menu on phones) lets anyone switch language, or
    back to English. Their own choice always wins and is remembered.
  - Arabic reads right to left.
  - Names, book titles, the company name and the six service names (Edition,
    Signal, Launch, Circle, Estate, Press) stay in English on purpose.
  - Tip: you can share a page in a set language by adding ?lang= to the link,
    for example  halcyonliterary.com/?lang=fr  or  halcyonliterary.com/?lang=yo

  Before relying on a language for clients, have a native speaker read it.
  This matters most for Yoruba, Swahili and Hindi.


FORMS  (sent with Resend)
  Both forms (consultation and roadmap) are sent by a small Cloudflare function
  (functions/api/contact.js) through your Resend account.
  - You receive each message at hello@halcyonliterary.com with exactly what the
    author wrote: name, email, book, stage, what they tried, their goal, the
    language they read the site in, and the page they sent it from.
  - Press Reply in your inbox and your answer goes straight to the author.
  - The author receives a confirmation email in their language. Roadmap
    requests also get the download button.
  - The thank-you message only appears once Resend has accepted the email. If
    sending fails, the visitor is asked to try again or email you directly.
  One-time setup:
    1. In Resend, add and verify the domain halcyonliterary.com.
    2. In Resend, create an API key (Sending access).
    3. In Cloudflare Pages > your project > Settings > Variables and Secrets,
       add a Secret named RESEND_API_KEY with that key, then redeploy.
    Optional variables: TO_EMAIL (default hello@halcyonliterary.com) and
    FROM_EMAIL (default Halcyon Literary House <hello@halcyonliterary.com>).


GOOGLE AND SHARING
  - After upload, add the site to Google Search Console
    (search.google.com/search-console) and submit:
    https://halcyonliterary.com/sitemap.xml
  - When the link is shared on WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn or X, it shows
    assets/og-image.jpg: your logo, headline and three covers.
