Founder & CEO, Book Marketing Strategist

Jennifer Lopez

Jennifer Lopez founded Halcyon Literary House with a simple belief: writing and publishing a book should not leave an author wondering how to find the people who would genuinely want to read it.

With 10 years of experience in book marketing, including six years in publishing, Jennifer has spent much of her professional life working with independent and self-published authors. Her background includes Amazon advertising, book positioning, listing optimization, reader acquisition, and building stronger author platforms.

Over the years, she came to recognize a recurring challenge. Authors were investing deeply in their books, yet many lacked the strategic support needed to navigate the increasingly competitive world of book marketing. Jennifer wanted to build a company that could approach those challenges with greater care, clarity, and individual attention.

That ambition became Halcyon Literary House.

Today, as Founder and CEO, Jennifer guides the company's vision while continuing to bring her marketing expertise to the work. She believes effective promotion begins with understanding the book, the audience it was written for, and the author's goals, rather than offering every writer the same solution.

Her purpose is to help authors make confident, informed decisions about their publishing journeys and give their books a stronger opportunity to find the readers they deserve.

Author Relations & Outreach Lead

Kate Ferguson

Kate researches authors and starts conversations where Halcyon may be able to help. She takes time to understand the book, the author’s goals and any concerns about working with a service provider. Kate explains the relevant support, answers questions about scope and next steps, and brings Jennifer or another specialist into the conversation when needed. She also helps with proposals and the handover, so authors know who they will be working with.

Book Production & Launch Preparation

Amanda K.

Amanda helps authors prepare for publication and organize the work leading up to a launch. She reviews what is ready, what still needs attention and how the book will be presented to readers. Working with Jennifer and the production specialists, she helps coordinate file preparation, book-page information and launch tasks. Her role gives authors a clearer schedule and someone to talk to when they are unsure what needs to happen next.

Author Branding & Creative Design

Jennifer O. Winters

Jennifer O. Winters helps authors with book covers and the visual materials around a release. She looks at the genre, the intended readers and the author’s ideas before developing a design. Her work can include author branding, promotional graphics, newsletter visuals and media-kit layouts. She works with the author through the agreed revisions, helping the cover and supporting materials feel consistent wherever readers encounter the book.

Author Websites & Email Systems

Adegbite Samuel

Adegbite handles the technology behind an author’s website and email list. He builds pages, connects signup forms and sets up the emails readers receive after subscribing. He also helps with landing pages, integrations and tracking. Authors work with Adegbite when they need a new system or help fixing an existing one. His role includes checking how the pieces work together and explaining how the author can use them after handover.

Reader Communities & Campaign Coordination

Sarah Kat

Sarah researches book clubs, reading groups and other communities where a book may be a good fit. She prepares introductions, follows up with organizers and helps coordinate discussions or author Q&As when a group agrees to take part. She also helps organize suitable newsletter placements as part of an agreed campaign. Sarah keeps authors informed about what has been proposed, which opportunities are confirmed and what they need to prepare.

PR & Author Visibility

Vivienne Ashcroft

Vivienne helps authors explain why their book or experience would interest a particular audience. She researches podcasts and media outlets, develops pitch angles and prepares materials such as author biographies, media kits and interview notes. She handles outreach and follow-up within the agreed scope. When an opportunity is confirmed, she helps the author understand the format and prepare for the conversation, keeping the message relevant to the host and their audience.

Video & Launch Content

Mike Gordon

Mike creates book trailers, launch videos and shorter clips for social platforms. He works from the book’s story, mood and the materials available, then plans the imagery, sound and pace. He can also work with Tattie on scripts and short-form ideas. Authors work with Mike to turn an agreed concept into video assets, review the edits and receive versions suited to the platforms they plan to use.
