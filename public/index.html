Process

Clear work, in the right order.

Every engagement begins with the one problem that limits everything after it.

  1. Private consultation

    Tell us about your book, where it stands, and what you want it to achieve.

  2. The assessment

    We study the book’s foundation, positioning, discoverability and path to readers.

  3. Your appointed team

    The specialists suited to your book take ownership of clear deliverables.

  4. The next chapter

    With the first constraint resolved, we plan what brings the next level of growth.
