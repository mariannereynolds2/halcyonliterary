EditionBook production

From manuscript to a book that looks ready to be bought.

A finished draft is not yet a finished book. Edition prepares your interior, cover and files for print and ebook to retailer standard, and guides every technical step of publishing, so your book arrives looking as if it belongs on the front table.

From document to edition.

  • Bleed, margin and KDP compliance
  • Publishing setup and upload guidance

SignalPositioning and discoverability

Make the right reader understand your book in seconds.

No amount of promotion can rescue a book that sends the wrong signal. Signal aligns your cover, description, categories, keywords and author story, so your book announces its genre, its promise and its reader before a single dollar is spent on reaching them.

Clear. Findable. Chosen.

  • Author positioning
  • Genre signal review
  • Goodreads setup and strategy
  • Series positioning
  • Relaunch diagnosis

CircleReader connections

Readers who talk about your book, with you in the room.

Conversations, not impressions.

  • Discussion coordination
  • Audience relationship strategy
